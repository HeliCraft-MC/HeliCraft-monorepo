## Release checks

Generation, TS7 build/typecheck, strict lint, formatting, Storybook, the Drizzle graph and all six Compose definitions pass. Root unit tests pass 202 cases; real PostgreSQL integration passes 77 cases in 31 suites. The final browser suites pass 49 distinct cases: 35 general cases, four company/FX journeys and ten public company cases. One production-asset case is intentionally skipped in development and passes in production. Integration and browser container lifecycles run sequentially to avoid Chromium network-change cancellation.

No schema push, reset or Minecraft EULA change occurred.

Local desktop/mobile screenshots are retained under the ignored `run/verification/` directory: company catalogue/profile, formation, finance, foreign branches/subsidiaries, shares, company tax treatment and FX. This folder also retains command logs and database checksum evidence. Screenshots show actual browser workflows and disposable data; they are not invented public world state.

## Deliberate V1 limits

Registered-company liquidation/capital retirement and paid securities trading are not implemented. Outstanding shares/assets prevent unsafe closure; private drafts may be withdrawn and branches closed through their respective procedures. There is no sector-specific company runtime, third-party application key system, physical geography, debt collection, credit, infinite FX liquidity or global exchange rate.

The reserve is protected on the existing bank account rather than moved to a separate escrow account. Expiry releases availability by the database clock; an explicit command can append expiry evidence. A below-minimum tail remains reserved and unavailable for fills until cancellation/expiry. These are explicit implemented semantics, not placeholders.

Future external applications, webhooks, typed economic events, additional assets/contracts/markets and Minecraft adapters can extend stable legal identities, Governance sources and grouped journals while preserving current authorization and immutable evidence.
