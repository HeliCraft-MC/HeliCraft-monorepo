# Taxation V1

Antares owns tax institutions, machine-readable laws, jurisdiction resolution, quotes and exact settlement. Vega composes the state, administration, personal-wallet and public interfaces through generated Alhena. Minecraft, physical buildings, geography, companies, exchange, residency, debt collection and arbitrary confiscation are outside V1.

## Institutions and authority

A state submits an immutable `ESTABLISH_TAX_OFFICE` economic directive with normalized localized name/charter. Its published Governance determines office approvals, citizen/council voting, combined evidence or a documented manual procedure. The existing monetary economic-directive mechanism is reused; no separate taxation roles, appointments, ballots or approval tables exist. An absent policy denies the action. Presets and published Governance revisions receive no additional rights automatically.

After state approval, the PENDING institution enters administrative review. `states.manage` may ratify or reject the exact applied establishment directive with a mandatory reason; ACTIVE → SUSPENDED and SUSPENDED → ACTIVE retain immutable review and audit history. A rejected application may be replaced on the same institution identity. Ratification concerns the web institution and makes no physical-building assertion. Charter changes require `CHANGE_TAX_OFFICE_CHARTER` through the same state decision mechanism.

Existing `ENACT_LAW` and `REPEAL_LAW` policies govern tax adoption/repeal; `AMEND_TAX_LAW` governs amendments. Constitution prose grants none of these capabilities. Each submitted directive fixes its payload hash, normalized text and Governance revision. Current active citizenship, uninterrupted unexpired office terms, current approvals, frozen generic votes and configured manual evidence are rechecked before execution. Editing a proposal requires a new immutable decision.

## Laws, events and jurisdiction

`tax_laws` supplies stable identity; `tax_law_revisions` appends numbered immutable revisions with the exact directive, normalized name/description/Markdown references, event/category, jurisdictions, rate, currency conditions and effective date. Repeal appends a REPEALED revision and retains the previous rule/text. Only the latest effective revision of a law applies. A scheduled amendment does not replace its predecessor early; a new revision cannot predate its predecessor. Effect begins no earlier than actual approval. No historical transfer is recalculated.

The typed executable registry contains only `PLAYER_TRANSFER`: a personal PLAYER-to-PLAYER wallet transfer. The sender is the taxpayer and pays tax in addition to the original minor-unit amount. The recipient receives the full principal. Emission, burning, bank/treasury transfers, tax payments, account creation and internal journal entries do not dispatch taxable events. Category codes distinguish independently enacted claims within this supported event. An empty currency condition covers every active currency; selected IDs restrict a law without exchanging currencies.

`TaxJurisdictionResolver` reads current active States memberships and returns separate evidence for CITIZENSHIP and TERRITORIAL. Multiple citizenships remain independent jurisdictions. TERRITORIAL explicitly uses `CITIZENSHIP_PROXY`; it never asserts coordinates or verified physical location. Evidence retains state, membership start time, basis and source. Several matching bases apply the same law once; different laws remain separate. Future verified territory/residency/company providers can extend this boundary without changing journal accounting.

## Exact arithmetic and treaties

Amounts use existing canonical integer-string minor units and server `bigint`; precision is two decimals. Rates are integral basis points, 0–10000 inclusive (0–100%). Each national tax is `floor(originalMinor × rateBps / 10000)`. A tiny result of zero retains its assessment and legal evidence but creates no zero posting. Principal plus all final taxes must fit the existing thirty-digit monetary limit. Intermediate multiplication uses arbitrary-precision bigint; database storage retains existing `numeric(38,0)` guarantees.

The engine first resolves every independent national claim, then applies active bilateral treaties to overlapping claims in the same category. Exclusive rights suppress only the covered other party's claims. A shared treaty replaces all covered national claims with one agreed rate on the original base. The first immutable party receives the floor of its agreed share; the second receives the exact remainder. Shares sum to 10000 basis points and allocations conserve every minor unit. Claims of third states and other categories remain independent. A treaty cannot create a claim when either party lacks an applicable national rule. Intersecting treaties are rejected at activation where possible and again deterministically at calculation. See [diplomacy](diplomacy.md).

## Quotes and settlement

`POST /account/wallet/quotes` derives the authenticated sender, verified recipient, memberships, effective laws and treaties on the server. Quotes are actor-bound, immutable, command-hashed, calculation-hashed and valid for five minutes. The UI displays principal, each law/revision and jurisdiction, treaty replacements, recipient treasuries, allocations, tax total and total debit before confirmation. Confirmation recalculates under the existing authorization/monetary locks and rejects changed parameters, actor, expiry or legal calculation. A new confirmation is required; the server never silently charges a different amount. Existing clients may omit a quote only when no national tax claim exists. Vega always obtains one.

One `economic_operations` row identifies the source event using the principal journal UUID. Its actor/idempotency key binds the complete financial command independently of quote renewal. Identical retries return the original journal before quote expiry/recalculation checks; changed commands conflict. `tax_assessments` retains every final charge and all national/treaty revision evidence; `tax_assessment_claims` gives each law a relational revision pin and enforces one law per event. `economic_journals` links the principal and every treasury allocation to the operation and assessment.

Settlement retains existing journals with exactly two opposite postings. The principal, all assessments, foreign-currency treasury account creation and every tax journal commit in one PostgreSQL transaction. There is no sequence of independent REST payments. Source funds are checked against total debit before posting; posting triggers and deferred integrity checks still prohibit negative ordinary balances, incomplete journals and supply drift. The new deferred operation check verifies that every positive allocation has its exact treasury journal, assessments match the recorded calculation, totals conserve value and all journals share the PostgreSQL transaction, actor and currency. Committed operations, assessment claims, quotes, journal links and revisions cannot be rewritten or extended. Tax journals bypass event dispatch, preventing recursive taxation.

The existing platform authorization advisory lock precedes monetary/state/account locks. Relevant citizenship states are locked in stable order and membership is reread so concurrent departure cannot leave ambiguous jurisdiction evidence. This intentionally serialized V1 favors financial correctness over throughput; scaling requires equivalent concurrency guarantees.

## Interfaces and privacy

State workspace sections `taxation` and `diplomacy` reuse LocalizableTextInput, FieldHelp, Dialog, exact MoneyAmount, shared state selection and the existing decision evidence/vote controls. Tax administration reuses the institution review form. No new shared design kit is introduced. Server-backed law examples explain one law; actual wallet quotes include all applicable laws/treaties.

Protected `/account/states/:stateId/taxation` exposes institution, numbered law history, decision evidence, foreign-currency treasury balances, actual tax receipts and per-currency totals. `/account/economic-operations/:id` exposes the saved complete calculation and journal links only to the taxpayer. Receipt history carries operation, assessment and journal identifiers with exact law/treaty revision links. Separate assessment history includes zero amounts and treaty exemptions. Law/treaty histories and treaty lists have bounded pages, with native GET links for public SSR. Public `/states/:slug/taxation`, `/tax-laws/:id`, `/treaties` and `/treaties/:id` render public policy/history and metadata with SSR, without forwarding cookies. Public tax DTOs exclude private transfers, taxpayers, administrative reasons, drafts and suspended/inactive state institutions; Markdown is rendered and sanitized by Antares.

## Storage and verification

Additive reviewed migrations `0016_taxation_diplomacy_v1.sql` and `0017_taxation_integrity.sql` add the new domain without rewriting earlier monetary history or weakening its triggers. Directive documents use a separate normalized reference table so the existing bank/currency payload schema remains compatible with the historical monetary migration test. Apply through the ordinary migrator to database `helicraft`; never use schema push or a destructive reset.

Validation evidence and remaining limits are tracked in [verification](taxation-verification.md). Future Minecraft services may submit authenticated, independently verified event/geography facts through REST. They must not set rates, claim arbitrary citizenship, bypass quote consent or write balances directly. Future companies/markets require registered typed events and verified context providers; their functionality is not advertised in V1.

Tax-office establishment now prefers the shared `CREATE_INSTITUTION` authority; the old published establishment-specific policy remains a fallback. Initial tax-office procedures can be configured in the establishment form and are published atomically in a new pinned governance/constitution revision. They cannot replace existing procedures or grant unrelated bank/diplomatic/governance powers. Further changes use the tax-office group in reforms. See [Governance](../states/governance.md).
