# Monetary V1 verification

Implementation date: 2026-10-17. Antares/PostgreSQL owns the monetary state; Vega, Atria and generated Alhena expose a complete web workflow without Minecraft.

## Delivered scope

- State-approved central bank establishment followed by a separate reasoned administrative ratification. Rejected applications, revised charters, suspension/restoration and administrative history retain their immutable evidence.
- One bank and one national currency per state, globally unique currency codes, immutable identities and normalized localized names/descriptions.
- Explicit immutable monetary policies: maximum supply, preceding 30-day gross issuance and maximum single issuance, each independently nullable. Issuance without an approved policy is denied. Actual rolling gross, remaining aggregate capacity, single-operation capacity, numbered revisions and their history are exposed by Antares and displayed in Vega.
- Exact integer-string amounts, currency precision two, bank/treasury/player/issuance accounts, atomic balanced postings, no negative ordinary balances and no automatic conversion.
- Separate authority for issuance, burning, bank transfers and treasury spending. Issuance credits the bank, never the initiator or treasury automatically. Player wallets are independent of citizenship and created by incoming transfers.
- Immutable directives, generic Voting with frozen electorates, single/any/all office approvals, combined procedures, current-term signature checks, explicit signature revocation and reasoned manual administration. Presets and old published revisions receive no new authority automatically.
- Protected state finances, personal wallets/transfers and transaction details; bank review and manual decision administration; public currency/bank/policy/issuance information rendered with SSR and no session forwarding.
- Per-page bounded private history and public issuance, cursor navigation for public currencies, accessible multilingual forms, FieldHelp explanations, existing player selection/identity, confirmation before submission and exact MoneyAmount presentation.

## Models, migrations and API

`0013_awesome_outlaw_kid.sql` creates ten monetary tables: `monetary_directives`, `monetary_approvals`, `monetary_votes`, `central_banks`, `bank_reviews`, `currencies`, `monetary_policy_revisions`, `financial_accounts`, `ledger_transactions`, `ledger_postings`. Constraints prevent duplicate institutions/currencies/wallets and cross-currency postings. Triggers protect submitted payloads, identities, policy/review history, committed journals and postings. Balances are derived transactionally from postings and checked again at commit.

`0014_monetary_supply_integrity.sql` also reconciles currency supply at every journal insertion, so an erroneous writer that posts balanced issuance but forgets the currency update cannot commit. Its PostgreSQL scenario checks complete rollback, including balances and journal count.

`0015_monetary_policy_revision_numbers.sql` adds immutable per-currency revision numbering, backfilling existing history in one locked transaction. The dedicated upgrade test migrates two old policy rows, compares their exact content/dates/identities, verifies the restored history guard and publishes revision 3.

All three migrations were applied to disposable PostGIS `helicraft` databases and to the configured local `helicraft` without reset or schema push. A read-only schema check confirms ten monetary tables and the added supply trigger; the final read-only check confirms `helicraft` and its policy `number` column. No gameplay fixture was inserted into the user's database.

Typed API declarations are in `apps/antares/src/modules/monetary/routes.ts`; accounting and authority remain in domain services. OpenAPI and Alhena are generated by `bun run generate`. Account routes expose state monetary workspaces, immutable directives with approve/revoke/apply/cancel, own wallets/transfers and frozen-voter payload access. Administrative routes expose bank reviews and pending monetary decisions. Public currency routes exclude private balances, transfers, review reasons and inactive-state configuration.

## UI

Atria adds one reusable primitive, MoneyAmount, in its own component directory, with exact formatting/parsing tests and default, large, English, negative and zero-balance Storybook states. Existing LocalizableTextInput, FieldHelp, Dialog, form controls and player identity/search are reused.

Vega adds bank, currency, monetary policy, issuance, bank finances and treasury sections to the current state workspace. Routes are `/app/wallet`, `/admin/central-banks`, `/admin/monetary-directives`, `/currencies` and `/currencies/:code`. Domain-specific composition stays in Vega; financial authority and computation stay in Antares.

## Operational limits and future integration

The deployment uses the existing global authorization advisory lock before monetary state/currency locks. Monetary commands and protected monetary reads are deliberately serialized in V1. This is an integrity-first throughput limit, not a bank independence flag. HTTP mutation limiting is process-local (300 per actor per 15 minutes), matching the existing platform limiter; multiple API replicas need a shared limiter or gateway protection. Future scaling must preserve lock ordering, current authority revalidation, idempotency and concurrent money invariants.

Minecraft building observations and authenticated plugin commands remain future REST integrations. No Deneb/Rigel implementation, physical building verification, exchange, reserve unit, market, loan, deposit, tax or production money faucet was introduced. Archive files and Minecraft plugins remain unchanged.
