## 1. General Provisions

1. The server is provided on an "as is" basis. The Administration does not guarantee uninterrupted operation or data safety.
2. The Administration reserves the right to block any player's access without prior notice or explanation.
3. Any changes, failures, or total loss of progress are possible. The server does not compensate for or restore lost resources.
4. The Administration determines punishments at its own discretion, using the rules only as a guideline.
5. Server rules may be changed by the Administration at any time without prior notice.
    1. New versions of the rules are announced in the official Telegram channel.
    2. Disagreement with new rules implies immediate termination of play on the server.
6. Players are required to know and follow the rules; ignorance of the rules is not an excuse.
7. By joining the server, a player automatically agrees to all rules and decisions of the Administration.
8. The player is solely responsible for the security and actions taken from their account.
9. Bans are applied by IP address. In the case of a shared IP, all users of that IP share responsibility.
10. For severe violations, the Administration has the right to clear inventory, delete buildings, and wipe player progress.

---

## 2. Griefing and PVP Rules ("Lore" System)

1. **Permission for Aggression:** Griefing and PVP are allowed on the server, but only with a valid in-game reason.
2. **Mandatory Telegram Registration:** Any planned attack, murder, or griefing of another player's base must be described in advance in the official Telegram channel under the **"Lore" (Лор)** thread.
    1. The player must state their nickname, the victim's nickname, and a detailed reason (e.g., revenge for theft, territorial conflict, unpaid debt).
    2. The action is considered legitimate only after it has been recorded in this thread.
    3. Killing or griefing without a record in the "Lore" thread is considered destructive behavior (RDM/Freegrief).
3. **Self-Defense:** In the event of a sudden attack, the player has the right to defend themselves and kill the attacker without prior recording in Telegram.
4. **Punishments for System Violations:**
    1. Attacking without a reason or without a "Lore" entry — account ban **from 3 days to 14 days**.
    2. Repeated violations or mass unjustified griefing — ban **from 30 days to permanent**.

---

## 3. Severe Violations

1. **Use of cheats or prohibited modifications (X-ray, fly, autoclickers, etc.):**
    1. Without significant damage — account ban **from 1 day to 4 weeks**.
    2. Medium damage — IP ban **from 2 weeks to 8 months**.
    3. Major damage — IP ban **from 2 months to permanent**.
2. **Threats to the server or Administration:**
    1. Verbal threats — mute or ban **from 2 hours to permanent**.
    2. Actual harmful actions — IP ban **from 2 weeks to permanent**.
3. **Exploiting server vulnerabilities (dupes, bugs, etc.):**  
   Permanent IP ban.
4. **Violation of the laws of the RF, Germany/EU, or the player's country of residence:**  
   Permanent ban without the possibility of appeal.
5. **Attempts to bypass bans:**  
   Banning of all known IPs and associated accounts of the player.

---

## 4. Other Violations

1. **Use of multi-accounts:**
    1. IP ban **from 1 week to 2 months**.
    2. Permanent ban of all additional accounts.
2. **Impersonating other players or Administration members:**
    1. Punishment — from a 2-hour mute to a 2-month IP ban.
3. **Real Money Trading (RMT):**  
   Permanent IP ban.

---

## 5. Chat and Behavior Rules

1. Political discussions, disputes, and propaganda — **mute from 30 days to permanent**.
2. Use of profanity (including masked) — **mute from 30 minutes to permanent**.
3. Sexual or 18+ content — **mute from 30 days to permanent**.
4. Discrimination (nationality, gender, orientation, etc.) — **mute from 30 days to permanent**.
5. Displaying Nazi symbols or similar ideologies — **mute from 60 days to permanent**.
6. Insulting players or the Administration — **mute from 2 days to permanent**.
7. Advertising third-party resources — **mute from 2 days to permanent**.

---

## 6. Receiving and Appealing Punishments

1. Vigilantism in any form is prohibited.
2. Appealing a punishment consists of two stages:
    1. Appeal to the Administrator who issued the punishment.
    2. If the result is unsatisfactory — appeal to the Head Administrator.
3. A punishment may be lifted if the conflict between players is fully settled in the "Lore" thread.

---

## 7. Rules for Administration

1. Abuse of power — **removal from office and ban from 7 days**.
2. Violation of server rules by an Administrator results in a punishment increased by 20-40% of the standard term.
3. Administrators are required to have and keep evidence of violations for forty-eight (48) hours.
4. Administrators are prohibited from disclosing players' personal data to third parties.
5. Selling administrative rights for real money is strictly prohibited.
